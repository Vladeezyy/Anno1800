Description
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This mod is an update of the original mod by nerses28 (www.nexusmods.com/anno1800/mods/351).
Since he has less time for modding, he allowed me to make an update.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The legendary specialists and items pack adds various legendary specialist and items. Use this mod only if you want to make your game easier (some items are very unbalanced).

Some items and specialists appear at pirates and Old Nate, the rest can be obtained as "normal" legendary items. Langs: English, Russian, Chinese, German. GUID-Range: 1900000000-1900000078.

List of items/specialist (traders):

Alexey Stakhanov - the legendary miner,
Captain Nemo  (Jean LaFortune, Anne Harlow) - great choice for expeditions,
Jacques Yves Cousteau (Old Nate) - perfect salvager specialist,
"Nobel" Shot (Jean LaFortune, Anne Harlow) - increases your military ships strength,
"Centurio" Gun (Jean LaFortune, Anne Harlow) - an ultimate gun,
The "Goliath" Engine (Old Nate) - for steamships,
The "Cyclone" Engine (Arctic Old Nate) - for airships,
Henry Ford - founder of the Ford Motor Company,
Elisha Graves Otis - inventor of a safety device that prevents elevators from falling,
Isaac Singer - made important improvements in the design of the sewing machine,
Claude Moët - vintner and wine merchant,
Coco Chanel - famous fashion designer,
Levi Strauss - manufacturer of blue jeans,
Mr. Pringle - you know him,
Ashot Chobanyan - shepherd,
Alfredo Linguini - famous cook from the cartoon "Ratatouille",
Methane Filtering System "Freshness" (Arctic Old Nate) - for coal mines,
Tunnel Boring Machine "Shai-Hulud" (Arctic Old Nate) - for arctic deep gold mines,
Heavy Drill "Shrew" (Arctic Old Nate) - for arctic deep gold mines.

New items:
Dmitri Mendeleev - Russian chemist and inventor, known for formulating the periodic law and creating the periodic table of elements;
Alexander Popov - Russian physicist, inventor of a radio receiving device;
Boris Yakobi (Moritz Hermann von Jacobi) - Russian engineer and inventor. He has created the first electric motor and developed diverse telephone devices;
Ivan Michurin - Russian biologist and practitioner of selection of new sorts of crop and fruit plants. He introduced over 300 new varieties;
Nikolai Vavilov - Russian and Soviet agronomist, botanist and geneticist. He created the world's first seedbank with 250,000 samples of seeds, roots and fruits;
Sergey Botkin - Russian clinician, therapist and activist, one of the founders of modern Russian medical science and education;
Alexander Laurant - Russian inventor. He has created the fire fighting foam and the world's first foam extinguisher;
Pavel Yablochkov - Russian electrical engineer, known as the inventor of the Yablochkov candle, a type of electric carbon arc lamp;
Vladimir Shukhov - Russian and Soviet engineer, scientist and architect. He has invented the first cracking method and created a new type of oil reservoir - cylindric Shukhov reservoir;
Andrey Vlasenko - Russian agrotechnician, inventor of a horsedrawn grain harvester;
Faddey Bellingshausen (Fabian Gottlieb von Bellingshausen) - Russian cartographer, explorer and naval officer, the discoverer of the continent of Antarctica;
Franz San Galli - Russian entrepreneur and inventor, creator of the first heating radiator;
Valiko Sunelishvili - The best spice and herb specialist.

If other mods are active, some items affect new buildings and/or give additional products (see screenshots). For now Noblesse oblige (Ricky&Taludas), Jakob's Industrial Cities, Jakob's New World Cities, Production Works (Kurila), Extended Enbesa (Kurila), The Wholesome Hacienda Overhaul (Taludas), Burrito Boom (Taludas), The True Jam Experience (Taludas), Enbesa Spectacles Production (Lion053), Arctic Variable Resources, Arctic Perfect Ending at Beginning, City Bakery, City Cobbler, Drakkam New Population Merchants, Sulfur Module (from Old World Paper Mill (Lion053)), Galena Module, Gunpowder Factory (from Military Attention), Suits, Cheese, Wine, Apples, Cherries, Tomatoes, Vegetables, Hemp, Flax, Spice Powder and Soda products have been taken into account. Compatibile with All Items Unlocked At Traders, NPC Dice Improved, lonelyPorter NPC Dice Reworked and Sting McRay Traders on Rarity mods.

Alexey Stakhanov also works for mines in Kingsgroove and Colossus of the New World: Finding Mayabeque :).

If you want the Arctic Old Nate's items to be discovered at the Research Institute, just uncomment research.include.xml on the top of the assets.xml file.

Known issues:

Although my mod works with Combat Overhaul: 01 Ships, the author of this mod intentionally changes values of some items from my mod (Captain Nemo, Jacques-Yves Cousteau, "Nobel" Shot, "Centurio" Gun, "Goliath" Engine, "Cyclone" Engine) as he likes. I will not mark the CO-mod as incompatible, but if you use it with mine, just know that the items mentioned will work otherways.
